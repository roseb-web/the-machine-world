## Overview

A glowing, pale mist drawn up from deep places. It lights the cities, drives the trains and fires the guns. Drawing stations pump it out of the ground, and the work pays well.

Workers who stay too long get **Lull Fatigue**: ringing ears, bad sleep, and cold grey patches of skin. Anyone can catch it. The Concord says it's like a cold: rest, and return to work.
