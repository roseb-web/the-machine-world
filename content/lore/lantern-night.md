## Overview

Thornwick's harvest festival, on the first new moon after harvest. Everyone lights a lantern "to keep the long night away". Old families have brass lanterns, handed down for generations. Everyone wears a moth mask. Then there's the bonfire, the food, and an old song with no words that everyone hums.

## What we know

### Session 1

The party won the cake-eating contest and the lantern race. A strange lamp-seller had a stall, only that night. The festival was interrupted by Wil Carder, who was taken away by Foreman Brede.
