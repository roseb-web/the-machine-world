## Overview

A flowing, hooked old writing found on ruins and the oldest graves. Reading it is against the law, unless you belong to Ordo Calathis. Teaching it to ordinary people is a crime. The citizenship test needs it, so only the right sort of people pass.

## What we know

### Session 3

The shapes thrown by an old brass lantern turned out to be letters of the Old Hand. See [[quests/the-lantern-hunt|The Lantern Hunt]].
