## What we know

### The Months

Common folk will simply say the season and the day, i.e Winter 12. The Concord, as usual, have their own way.

### The Stars

Twelve constellations: **old gods, heroes of legend, and great beasts long dead.** 
- **Where you live decides which ones you know.** Some can only be seen from one part of Vaharan. A northerner knows Vahra, the Dragon. A Mire child has never seen it.
- **They're also star signs.** Each one rules 25 days of the year. Whichever one you're born under is your sign.
- Varrow's smog and Lull light hide most stars. City people only know the brightest few.
