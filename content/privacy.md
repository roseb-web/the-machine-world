**The Machine World** is a personal wiki for a tabletop roleplaying campaign. It's run by one person, for a small group of players.

## What this site collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, logins, forms, cookies or analytics, and I don't collect or store any information about visitors.

## Services the site uses

Your browser loads parts of the site from these services. Each has its own privacy policy, and may see your IP address when you visit:

- **GitHub Pages** hosts the site.
- **Google Fonts** and **jsDelivr** supply fonts and page styles.
- **Pinterest** supplies the moodboard images, which are loaded from Pinterest's servers.

## The Pinterest app

The site's Pinterest app is **read only**. It reads the Pins on one board that I collaborate on, and shows them as moodboards on this wiki. Each image links back to its Pin on Pinterest.

The app:

- uses only the permissions to read boards and Pins (`boards:read`, `pins:read`)
- doesn't create, edit, save or delete anything on Pinterest
- doesn't access, collect or store any data about other Pinterest users
- keeps its access token privately on my own computer, never on this website
