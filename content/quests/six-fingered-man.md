## Overview

Lanterns keep going missing in [[places/the-marrowlands/thornwick/index|Thornwick]]. Not all of them were taken by the cat. Something else walks on the roofs at night.

## What we know

### Session 3

Beside [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/biscuit|Biscuit]]'s nest on the chapel roof, the party found sooty handprints. Long and thin, with six fingers. Far too big for a cat.

### Session 4

The party told [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/mother-wren|Mother Wren]] about the roof. *"My grandmother said the light keeps the man off the roof."* She was vague about the soot marks.

### Session 5

Wren gave Fen back the race lantern from the inn roof. *"Be wary that he will come."*
