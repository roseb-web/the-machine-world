## Overview

[[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/jory-banks|Jory]] asked the party to find out what was walking on the roof of [[places/the-marrowlands/thornwick/the-banked-fire|The Banked Fire]] at night. Lanterns had been going missing all over the village.

## What we know

### Session 2

Jory was tired out. Something had been on his roof every night. The party agreed to look, and he offered them a warm meal for it.

### Session 3

It was a cat: [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/biscuit|Biscuit]]. The party chased him from the inn roof to the chapel roof, and caught him. In his nest they found a brass lantern, and sooty six-fingered handprints. Biscuit has been with the party ever since.

### Session 5

Jory baked them a pie. The party have his favour.
