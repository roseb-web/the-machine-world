## Overview

Since the Concord built its drawing station on the hill above [[places/the-marrowlands/thornwick/index|Thornwick]], the cattle have sickened, the crops have failed, and everyone dreams the same dream. Workers who fall ill with *Lull Fatigue* aren't sent home. The Concord says it's like a cold. The party means to find out what's going on up the hill, and get the sick out.

## What we know

### Session 2

The party visited the station. The guards carry Lull guns. In a shed out the back, sick workers are kept away from everyone. Enok said little, but agreed that things do seem tied to the station. There was moaning from another room. "It's the pipes," said Enok. Fen took a fever list from the desk.

[[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/reeve-tomas-ashdown|Reeve Tomas Ashdown]] says it's just a bad cold. Nobody believes him. He says Wil Carder was taken back to the shed.

[[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/jory-banks|Jory Banks]] has been told to get rooms ready: a company of soldiers is on its way, and a Warden-Inspector will stay at [[places/the-marrowlands/thornwick/the-banked-fire|The Banked Fire]].

### Session 4

[[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/mother-wren|Mother Wren]] calls what the station is digging up "filth", and says it should burn to the ground. *"The danger is up the hill, child."*

### Session 5

[[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/pell|Pell]] showed them a list of sick workers who are being taken away, [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/wil-carder|Wil Carder]] among them. It's signed "O. B."

Pell offered a way into the station, in trade for the lantern from the chapel roof. He led the party into a tunnel, and left them at its last bend.
