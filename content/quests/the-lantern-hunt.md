## Overview

When an old brass lantern is lit, its pattern throws shapes on the walls, and some of those shapes are letters of the Old Hand. Different lanterns throw different letters. Collect enough, and the party could read what nobody is allowed to read.

**Letters so far:** K, E, P, J, Q, Z, S, I, N

## What we know

### Session 3

The party lit the brass lantern they won in the race. Its shapes matched the marks on Wil's note: **K, E, P**. The lantern from the chapel roof gave **J, Q, Z**.

### Session 5

[[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/pell|Pell]] read the notice board note for them, and gave them a few more letters: **S, I, N**. Neri used the letters to read the writing on the old stone in the grave, and to work on Wil's note. The party gave him the lantern from the chapel roof in trade.

[[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/mother-wren|Mother Wren]] had taken the race lantern down from the inn roof. She gave it back to the party: *"Be wary that he will come."*

[[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/mrs-agnes-hesk|Mrs Agnes Hesk]] sent them to [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/old-corwin|Old Corwin]], rather than lend her own lantern. *"In the big city they have actual lamps now."*
