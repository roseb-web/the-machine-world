## Overview

A poor farming village in The Marrowlands, tucked into a fold of wooded hills. A Concord Lull drawing station sits on the hill above it. It's new, and it brought jobs and money.

Since the station opened: sick cattle, failing crops, and everyone dreaming the same dream.

**Places:** [[places/the-banked-fire|The Banked Fire]] (inn), Odds and Needs (general store, run by Mrs Agnes Hesk), [[people/mother-wren|Mother Wren]]'s chapel, the graveyard (Old Corwin), the mill, and the drawing station.

## What we know

### Session 1

Lantern Night. Lanterns everywhere, moth masks, a lantern race and a cake-eating contest (both won by the party). During the festival, Wil Carder was taken away by Foreman Brede. That night, the party fought three stalk-men.

### Session 2

The party visited the drawing station. The guards carry Lull guns. Sick workers are kept in a shed out the back. Reeve Tomas Ashdown says it's just a bad cold. Nobody believes him.

### Session 3

The party chased Biscuit the cat from the inn roof to the chapel roof. There they found sooty six-fingered handprints and a brass lantern.
