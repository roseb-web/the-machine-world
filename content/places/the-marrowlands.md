## Overview

Farmland and small villages near the border. Poor, careful people who don't trust machines or the city. Most folk follow the Faith of the Long Night.

On paper, the Marrowlands has belonged to Varrow since the old border wars ended, forty years ago. In practice, the villages run themselves, and each one elects a reeve every year.

**Places:** [[places/thornwick|Thornwick]], [[places/fennicks-ford|Fennick's Ford]], and [[places/harrowgate|Harrowgate]] just over the border.
