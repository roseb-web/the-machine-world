## Overview

A poor farming village in [[places/the-marrowlands/index|The Marrowlands]], tucked into a fold of wooded hills. A Concord Lull drawing station sits on the hill above it. It's new, and it brought jobs and money.

Since the station opened: sick cattle, failing crops, and everyone dreaming the same dream.

**Places:** [[places/the-marrowlands/thornwick/the-banked-fire|The Banked Fire]] (inn), [[places/the-marrowlands/thornwick/odds-and-needs|Odds and Needs]] (general store, run by [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/mrs-agnes-hesk|Mrs Agnes Hesk]]), [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/mother-wren|Mother Wren]]'s chapel, the graveyard ([[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/old-corwin|Old Corwin]]), the mill, and the drawing station.

## What we know

### Session 1

Lantern Night. Lanterns everywhere, moth masks, a lantern race and a cake-eating contest (both won by the party). During the festival, [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/wil-carder|Wil Carder]] was taken away by Foreman Brede. That night, the party fought three stalk-men.

### Session 2

The party visited the drawing station. The guards carry Lull guns. Sick workers are kept in a shed out the back. [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/reeve-tomas-ashdown|Reeve Tomas Ashdown]] says it's just a bad cold. Nobody believes him.

### Session 3

The party chased [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/biscuit|Biscuit]] the cat from the inn roof to the chapel roof. There they found sooty six-fingered handprints and a brass lantern.
