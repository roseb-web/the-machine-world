## Overview

The Concord's Lull drawing station, on the hill above [[places/the-marrowlands/thornwick/index|Thornwick]]. Built three months ago. It brought jobs and money, and the village is grateful. Its lamps burn a cold, steady purple.

## What we know

### Session 2

The guards carry Lull guns. Sick workers are kept in a shed out the back. There was moaning from another room. "It's the pipes," said Enok. Fen took a fever list and an engineer's drawing from the desk.
