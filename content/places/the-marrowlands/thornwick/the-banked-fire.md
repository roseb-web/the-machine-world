## Overview

The biggest building in [[places/the-marrowlands/thornwick/index|Thornwick]], run by [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/jory-banks|Jory Banks]]. Warm and busy, with herbs hanging from the beams and a long scaled creature carved along the great beam over the bar.

Four guest rooms upstairs, named after card suits:
- **Heart:** kept for the Warden-Inspector
- **Diamond:** "Mr Penn" ([[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/pell|Pell]])
- **Club:** empty
- **Spade:** the party

## What we know

### Session 2

Jory is tired out. Something has been on the roof at night. A soldier warned him to get rooms ready: a company is coming, and the Warden-Inspector will stay at the inn.

### Session 3

The thing on the roof turned out to be a cat, [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/biscuit|Biscuit]]. The party chased it across to the chapel roof. Luca spotted a small paper boat in the pond behind the inn.

### Session 5

Jory's pie arrived, decorated with two lopsided six-pointed stars. The party have earned Jory's favour.
