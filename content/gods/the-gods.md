## Overview

People pray to old gods and new gods.

**The old gods** are prayed to in the countryside. Their real names are forgotten, and their faces were scraped off every statue long ago.
- [[gods/the-one-who-sleeps|The One Who Sleeps]]: rest, and the dark below
- [[gods/the-lantern-bearer|The Lantern-Bearer]]: light and safety
- [[gods/the-last-sheaf|The Last Sheaf]]: the harvest

**The new gods** belong to the cities and the war. Their pictures are everywhere.
- [[gods/lucastra|Lucastra]], the Kindled: life everlasting
- [[gods/ferrox|Ferrox]], the Iron Mouth: war
- [[gods/mutatrix|Mutatrix]], the Mother of Engines: change
