## Overview

An old god of light and safety, remembered on Lantern Night. Her real name is forgotten.

## What we know

### Session 2

A folk-song collector's notes (found on the road back from the station) list three customs found all over the Marrowlands, that nobody can explain: a lamp-box kept lit where someone died on the road, a lantern set on every grave on Lantern Night, and a bead hung on a faceless statue after a bad night survived. The oldest statues hold a lantern up in front of them. The collector believed Lantern Night was once her festival.
