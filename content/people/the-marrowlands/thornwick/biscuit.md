## Overview

A big ginger tom, and not quite an ordinary one. Copper veins glow faintly under his fur, his tail is like jointed wire, and his eyes shine like two lamps. He purrs in three notes, and likes to curl up round a lantern.

## What we know

### Session 3

Biscuit turned out to be the thing on the Banked Fire's roof. The party chased him across the rooftops to the chapel roof, and caught him. He's been with them ever since.

### Session 5

Pell asked if the party had met Biscuit: *"Shame about the Thrum."* Biscuit moved into Luca's family home with the party.
