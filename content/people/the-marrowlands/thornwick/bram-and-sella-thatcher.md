## Overview

**Sella:** small, sharp and furious. Her family's cattle were the first in Thornwick to change, and nobody will listen to her. Both her forearms are bandaged. *"Look me in the eye and tell me it's bad feed."*

**Bram:** Sella's husband. Big hands, a quiet voice, and not many words. He used to play the lute at every harvest supper.
