## Overview

Round, red-faced and always a little out of breath, in robes far too grand for a village. He signed the deal that brought the station to Thornwick. *"Now, now, no need to go stirring things up!"*

## What we know

### Session 2

Says the sickness is just a bad cold. Nobody believes him. He said Wil Carder was taken back to the shed.
