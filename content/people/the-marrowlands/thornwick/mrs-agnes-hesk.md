## Overview

Tiny, sharp-eyed and always knitting, with far too much jewellery for a village shopkeeper. Loves gossip, and will trade a discount for a good story. *"I'll knock a bit off for a good story."*

## What we know

### Session 1

Ran the cake-eating contest on Lantern Night. Fen noticed she seemed sad.

### Session 3

Told the party Tam's mother, Hettie, does the washing down by the mill.

### Session 5

Neri and Luca asked about her pendant, a smooth stone that shimmers purple and green. Her grandson Danny gave it to her. He said he found it at work. She pointed them to Old Corwin about the lanterns. *"In the big city they have actual lamps now."*
