## Overview

A tall figure in tattered robes, with a mask of thin metal bars where a face should be. Sells lanterns in colours that don't have names. *"Ah. You came."*

## What we know

### Session 1

The party bought trinkets at his stall: a mechanical goldfish in an orb, a silver teardrop earring, and a gem like a lump of coal. He creeped them out.
