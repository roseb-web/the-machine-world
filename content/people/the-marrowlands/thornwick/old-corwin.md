## Overview

The oldest man in Thornwick, bent nearly double over his spade. He looks after the chapel and the graveyard, and talks to the dead by their first names. *"Ah, that one's Eliza. Sharp one, she was."*

## What we know

### Session 3

The party met him in the graveyard after a hard fight, and promised to come back and help him with a grave.

### Session 4

The party did the grave-digging job, and refused payment.
