## Overview

A thin, pale young man who works at the station. Shy and polite, when he's well.

## What we know

### Session 1

Interrupted the Lantern Night festival, and was taken away by Foreman Brede. The party ended up with a scrap of Old Hand from him.

### Session 2

The Reeve says he was taken back to the shed.

### Session 5

Wil's name is on [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/pell|Pell]]'s list of sick workers who are being taken away. At the bottom: "O. B.", which looks like a signature.
