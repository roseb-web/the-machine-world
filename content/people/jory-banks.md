## Overview

A very tall harengon with ears that swing round the moment you walk in. A cheerful host, good with names, and serious fast if trouble starts. *"Not in here, friends. Take it outside."*

## What we know

### Session 1

Asked the party for honey cakes, and they delivered.

### Session 2

Tired out. Something has been on his roof at night, and the party agreed to look. A soldier told him to get rooms ready for a company of soldiers, with the Warden-Inspector staying at the inn.

### Session 5

Baked them a pie. The party have earned his favour.
