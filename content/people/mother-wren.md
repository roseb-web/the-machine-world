## Overview

The tall, barefoot old woman who keeps Thornwick's chapel. She always carries a lit lantern, even in daylight, and talks like she's giving a sermon. She reads people's dreams. She follows a faith called Dolvenya.

## What we know

### Session 4

The party visited the chapel and told her about the disturbance on the roof. *"My grandmother said the light keeps the man off the roof."* She was vague about the soot marks, and kept turning the talk to the station: *"The danger is up the hill, child."* She called what they're digging up "filth", and said the station should burn to the ground.

She read Nix's dreams and called him "lost child". She told him he will lose more if he does not act tonight.
