## Overview

The Meridian Concord rules Varrow, the great machine city. Every Concord official wears the same grey coat, so people call them **the grey coats**. Their sign is the Watching Eye, an eye inside a gear. Their saying: *"Lull is light. Light is order."*

The Concord runs the drawing stations that pump Lull out of the ground, and pays its workers in scrip that only works in Concord stores. It has been at war with the Ostrand Empire for twelve years. To be a citizen of Varrow, you must pass a test, and the test needs the Old Hand. Lately, Concord recruiters have been touring the villages, paying young people a silver shilling to sit it.
