## Overview

A proud, old mountain empire to the east. It has had no emperor for sixty years: its old noble houses rule it together. Varrow was once an Ostrand city, until it grew rich on Lull and broke away about a hundred years ago. Ostrand has never forgiven it, and calls this year 640, not 100.

The Concord and Ostrand have been at war for twelve years, along the Pale Front.
