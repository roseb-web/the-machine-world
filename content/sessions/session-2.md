## Overview

A visit to the drawing station, and a lot of questions nobody wants to answer.

- [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/bronwyn|Bronwyn]] made very bad porridge. Luca took his father's dinner up.
- Luca told the party he's a warlock. Neri explained what stalk-men are.
- The party took the lamp-seller's lantern, collected the brass hearts, and took some job cards from the notice board, including one in Old Hand.
- At [[places/the-marrowlands/thornwick/the-drawing-station|the drawing station]], the guards carry Lull guns. Round the back, in the shed, they found [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/enok|Enok]] and the other sick workers. Enok said things do seem connected to the station. Moaning came from another room: *"It's the pipes."*
- Fen took a fever list and an engineer's drawing from the desk.
- On the road back, they found a folk-song collector's notes, and returned a lost dog, Nettles.
- [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/reeve-tomas-ashdown|The Reeve]] said the sickness is just a bad cold. Nobody believed him. He said Wil had been taken back to the shed.
- [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/jory-banks|Jory]] asked them to look into something on his roof. Soldiers are coming, and the Warden-Inspector will stay at the inn.
