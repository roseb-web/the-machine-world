## Overview

The party met the man in the Diamond room, and went looking in it.

- At breakfast, they met [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/pell|Pell]], staying at the inn as "Mr Penn". At first they thought he was a cop.
- [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/dunny-pratt|Dunny Pratt]] burst in and accused Pell of scamming him.
- Jory said Pell asks about papers, has inky hands, and gossips with the old ladies of the village.
- While Jory was busy with a pie, the party searched Pell's room. They found travel papers and a stub of white chalk, noted what they found, and put everything back.
- At the chapel, [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/mother-wren|Mother Wren]] taught them the word **Dolvenya**. *"The danger is up the hill, child."*
- The party did Old Corwin's grave-digging job, and refused payment.
- Pell cornered them. He hadn't known for sure that they'd been in his room, until they gave it away. *"You wanna see what I found?"*
