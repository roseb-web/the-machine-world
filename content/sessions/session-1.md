## Overview

Lantern Night in Thornwick, and everyone woke from the same dream.

- [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/jory-banks|Jory]] asked for honey cakes, so the party bought twelve at [[places/the-marrowlands/thornwick/odds-and-needs|Odds and Needs]].
- [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/dunny-pratt|Dunny Pratt]] tried to pick Fen's pocket. Luca warned him off.
- At the [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/the-lamp-seller|lamp-seller]]'s stall, they bought a mechanical goldfish in an orb, a silver teardrop earring, and a gem like a lump of coal. He creeped them out.
- The party won the cake-eating contest and the lantern race, and the prize was an old brass lantern.
- [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/wil-carder|Wil Carder]] burst into the festival. [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/foreman-oskar-brede|Foreman Oskar Brede]] took him away. The party ended up with a scrap of Old Hand.
- That night, they fought three stalk-men, and found a note.
- They slept at Luca's family home.
