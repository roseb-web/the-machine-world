## Overview

Lanterns that throw letters, a fight in the graveyard, and a cat on the roof.

- [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/mrs-agnes-hesk|Mrs Hesk]] sent the party to Hettie, who does the washing by the mill. They gave her Tam's letter. Tam is gone. He left a daughter, who's two.
- The party lit the brass lantern. Its light threw letters of the Old Hand: **K, E, P**. See [[quests/the-lantern-hunt|The Lantern Hunt]].
- In the graveyard, hands came up out of the ground. After a hard fight, they met [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/old-corwin|Old Corwin]], and promised to help him with a grave tomorrow.
- At [[places/the-marrowlands/thornwick/the-banked-fire|The Banked Fire]], they saw the Thatchers, [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/pell|Pell]] and [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/garran-tull|Garran Tull]].
- The thing on the roof was a cat, [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/biscuit|Biscuit]]. They chased him to the chapel roof, where they found sooty six-fingered handprints and a brass lantern.  It gave **J, Q, Z**.
- Luca spotted a small paper boat in the pond behind the inn.
