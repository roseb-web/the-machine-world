## Overview

Pell showed his hand, then left town.

- Pell passed them the Spade room key, and showed them his list.
- He reads a little Old Hand. He admitted he wrote the note on the notice board. Asked who "we" are, he said it's a bit more than tieflings. He gave them a few more letters.
- He wants a way into the station, and will trade for it. *"Anyone who tries to control knowledge is only interested in segregation."* He shipped out that night.
- Jory's pie came, with two lopsided six-pointed stars on top. The party have Jory's favour.
- The party moved their things, and Biscuit, into Luca's family home.
- Fen visited [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/mother-wren|Mother Wren]]. She gave back the race lantern, which she'd found on the chapel roof.
- Neri and Luca asked [[people/the-marrowlands/thornwick/mrs-agnes-hesk|Mrs Hesk]] about her pendant. Her grandson Danny gave it to her: he found it at work.
- Pell took the Reeve's lantern. The party followed his route out of town, until he left them at the final bend.
